Privacy Policy

Bluxury · Last updated: 21 November 2026

Bluxury ("we", "us", "our") provides a WhatsApp messaging service that real-estate developers and their sales teams use to share project information with interested buyers, answer enquiries, and book site visits. This policy explains what information we collect through this service, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you receive or reply to one of our WhatsApp messages, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the name you provided to the developer or that appears on your WhatsApp profile.
  • Your replies and selections, such as the property configuration you are interested in (e.g. 2BHK / 3BHK), your budget range, and your preferred site-visit date and time.
  • Message status information provided by WhatsApp, such as whether a message was delivered or read, and the time it was sent.

We do not ask for or collect payment details, government ID numbers, or passwords through this service.

2. How we use your information

  • To send you information about the real-estate project you enquired about, including brochures and location details.
  • To understand your requirements (configuration and budget) and connect you with the right sales representative.
  • To schedule and confirm site visits and notify the developer's sales team about your booking.
  • To respect your opt-out and avoid sending you further messages once you ask us to stop.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix technical problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising unrelated to your enquiry.

3. Who we share it with

  • The real-estate developer whose project you enquired about, so their sales team can follow up with you.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp), which delivers messages between you and us under the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Service providers that host our servers, databases and documents (such as cloud hosting and file storage providers), who process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities, if we are required to do so by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry information for as long as it is needed to respond to your enquiry and for the developer's sales follow-up, and in any case for no longer than 24 months after your last interaction, unless a longer period is required by law. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see section 6).

5. How to stop receiving messages

Reply STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE or NOT INTERESTED to any of our WhatsApp messages at any time. We will mark your number as opted out and will not send you further updates about that project. You can also block the number in WhatsApp.

6. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, in line with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

To request deletion of your data:

Email us at privacy@bluxury.shop with the subject "Data deletion request" and the WhatsApp number our messages were sent to.

We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

7. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted administrator access to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a data breach where required by law.

8. Children

This service is intended for adults interested in purchasing property. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
